An undergraduate look at the working economy.

The Washington University Journal of Economics is a student publication that covers the state of the economy at three scales: the St. Louis local economy, the United States national economy, and the international economy. Our writers review the data releases, policy decisions, and market moves that shape the moment.

We publish on a rolling basis rather than in fixed editions, so reviews go up as they are finished. Recent work covers artificial intelligence and the economy, Missouri House Bill 3231, and the crypto-asset economy.

Jack Powers and John Haase take the case of Nigeria, Africa's largest crypto market, and find that stablecoin demand responds to Naira devaluation and cools inflation in the short run, while pressure on banking deposits runs only indirectly.
